# Aredvi Sura Anahita (The Immaculate Lady of the Flowing Waters and Chariot of Four Steeds)

**Culture**: Persian & Iranian | **Epoch**: -1000 to 650
**Archetype**: Goddess of Rivers, Fertility, and Royal Sovereignty Riding Wind and Cloud

## Scholarly Synopsis

Anahita is an Iranian goddess. She is praised in the Aban Yasht, one of the Zoroastrian hymns contained in the Avesta, under the name Aradvi Sura Anahita. In Middle Persian, her name was rendered Ardwisur Anahid.
