# Zahhak the Serpent King and Kaveh's Uprising

**Culture**: Persian & Iranian | **Epoch**: -800 to 1000
**Archetype**: Shoulder Serpent Tyrant / Blacksmith's Apron of Rebellion

## Scholarly Synopsis

Zahhak or Zahhāk is an evil figure in Persian mythology and folklore, evident in ancient Persian folklore as Aži Dahāka, the venomous serpent king who sprouted two voracious serpents from his shoulders and was overthrown by the blacksmith Kaveh and hero Fereydun.
