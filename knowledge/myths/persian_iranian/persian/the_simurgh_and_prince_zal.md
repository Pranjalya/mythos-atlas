# The Simurgh and Prince Zal

**Culture**: Persian & Iranian | **Epoch**: -500 to 1000
**Archetype**: Benevolent Mythic Avian Guardian of the Hero

## Scholarly Synopsis

The simurgh is a benevolent bird in Iranian mythology and literature. It bears some similarities with mythological birds from different origins, such as the phoenix and the humā. The figure can be found in all periods of Iranian art and literature and is also evident in the iconography of Georgia, medieval Armenia, the Eastern Roman Empire, and other regions that were within the realm of Persian cultural influence.
