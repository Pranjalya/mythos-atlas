# Haft Peykar (Bahram Gur and the Seven Pavilions of the Planets)

**Culture**: Persian & Iranian | **Epoch**: 1150 to 1200
**Archetype**: Astrological Journey through Seven Colored Domes of Transcendent Wisdom

## Scholarly Synopsis

Haft Peykar, also known as Bahramnameh, is a romantic epic poem by Persian poet Nizami Ganjavi, written in 1197. This poem is one of his five works known collectively as Khamsa ("Quintet").
