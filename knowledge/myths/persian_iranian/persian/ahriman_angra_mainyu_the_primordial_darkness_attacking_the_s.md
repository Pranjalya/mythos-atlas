# Ahriman / Angra Mainyu (The Primordial Darkness Attacking the Sacred Bull)

**Culture**: Persian & Iranian | **Epoch**: -1200 to 650
**Archetype**: Active Cosmic Adversary Poisoning the Pure Waters, Plants, and Beasts

## Scholarly Synopsis

In Zoroastrianism, Ahriman, also written Ahreman or as Ahrimanes, is the destructive evil spirit who functions as the main adversary either of the Spenta Mainyu or directly of Ahura Mazda, the highest Zoroastrian deity. His Avestan name is Angra Mainyu.
