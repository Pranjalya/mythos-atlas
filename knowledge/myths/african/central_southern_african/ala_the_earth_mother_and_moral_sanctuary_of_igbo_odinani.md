# Ala (The Earth Mother and Moral Sanctuary of Igbo Odinani)

**Culture**: West African | **Epoch**: 900 to 1900
**Archetype**: Goddess of the Sacred Soil and Ancestral Womb Enforcing Omenala (Moral Law)

## Scholarly Synopsis

Ala is the female Alusi (deity) of the earth, morality, fertility, and creativity in Odinani. In Odinani, Ala rules over the underworld and holds the deceased ancestors in her womb. Her name literally translates to "Earth" in the Igbo language, denoting her powers over the earth and her status as the ground itself. Ala is considered the highest Alusi in the Igbo pantheon.
