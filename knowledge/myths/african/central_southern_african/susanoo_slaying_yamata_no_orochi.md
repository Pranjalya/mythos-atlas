# Susanoo slaying Yamata no Orochi

**Culture**: East Asian | **Epoch**: 500 to 712
**Archetype**: Storm God Slaying Eight-Headed Dragon for Sacred Sword

## Scholarly Synopsis

Yamata no Orochi  is a legendary eight-headed and eight-tailed serpent that appears in Japanese mythology. Both the Kojiki and Nihon Shoki record that the kami Susanoo no Mikoto, in order to rescue Kushinadahime, defeated the serpent and that the Kusanagi no Tsurugi, part of the Imperial Regalia of Japan, was found within the serpent's tail.
