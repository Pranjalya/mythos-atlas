# Phra Lak Phra Lam (The Lao Ramayana along the Mekong River)

**Culture**: Southeast Asian | **Epoch**: 1350 to 1800
**Archetype**: Epic Brothers Protecting Royal Honor Transposed onto the Tropical Mekong

## Scholarly Synopsis

Phra Lak Phra Ram is the national epic of the Lao people, an adaptation of the ancient Indian epic Ramayana.
