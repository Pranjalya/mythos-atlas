# Curupira (The Backward-Footed Forest Protector of the Amazon)

**Culture**: Andean & South American | **Epoch**: 1000 to 1900
**Archetype**: Fiery-Haired Forest Guardian Misleading Poachers with Reverse Footprints

## Scholarly Synopsis

The Curupira, Currupira or Korupira is a forest spirit in the myth of the Tupí-Guaraní speaking areas in the Brazil, Paraguay and Guyanas. It is a guardian of the forests that punishes humans for overcutting.
