# El Dorado and the Sacred Raft of Lake Guatavita (Muisca)

**Culture**: Andean & South American | **Epoch**: 800 to 1500
**Archetype**: Gilded King Submerging into High-Mountain Sacred Water

## Scholarly Synopsis

El Dorado is a mythical city of gold supposedly located somewhere in South America. The legend originated in 16th-century Spanish accounts of a ruler known as el Dorado, or the Golden One, who was said to cover himself in gold dust before washing it off in a sacred lake. Over time, the name came to refer not only to the ruler, but also to a lost city or kingdom of immense wealth.
