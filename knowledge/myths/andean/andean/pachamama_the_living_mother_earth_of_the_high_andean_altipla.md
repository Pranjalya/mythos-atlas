# Pachamama (The Living Mother Earth of the High Andean Altiplano)

**Culture**: Andean & South American | **Epoch**: 500 to 1900
**Archetype**: Cosmic Mother Living in Mountain Terrains Requiring Reciprocal Offerings (Pago)

## Scholarly Synopsis

Pachamama or Mama Pacha is the Andean deity representing space-time, revered by the peoples of the Andes. In Inca mythology and religion, she is a "mother goddess" type deity, representing the universal energy that connects everything, linked to space, time, the universe, totality, life, fertility, and nature. She is considered an omnipresent deity with creative power, capable of sustaining life in the cosmos. The four cosmological Quechua principles—Water, Earth, Sun, and Moon—claim Pachamama as their prime origin.
