# Boitatá (The Giant Luminous Bull-Serpent of Phosphorescent Fire)

**Culture**: Andean & South American | **Epoch**: 1000 to 1900
**Archetype**: Giant Eye-Filled Fire Serpent Guarding Forests from Incendiary Intruders

## Scholarly Synopsis

Boitatá, in Brazilian native folklore, refers to either a will-o'-the-wisp, a mythical fire snake which guards against humans setting fire to the fields or forests, or a bull-like creature.
