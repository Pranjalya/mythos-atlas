# Baldr's Death and the Mistletoe

**Culture**: Norse & Germanic | **Epoch**: 800 to 1200
**Archetype**: Death of the Pure Light God / Foretoken of Doom

## Scholarly Synopsis

Baldr in Old Norse, Bældæġ in Old English, Balder or Palter in Old High German, is a god in Germanic mythology. In Norse mythology, he is a son of the god Odin and the goddess Frigg, and has numerous brothers, such as Thor and Váli.
