# Thor's Fishing Trip for Jormungandr

**Culture**: Norse & Germanic | **Epoch**: 700 to 1200
**Archetype**: Thunder God vs Midgard Serpent

## Scholarly Synopsis

In Norse mythology, Jörmungandr, also known as the Midgard Serpent or World Serpent, is an unfathomably large and monstrous sea serpent who dwells in the world sea, encircling the Earth (Midgard) and biting his own tail, an example of an ouroboros. As a result of him surrounding Midgard, the beast is referred to as the World Serpent. Jörmungandr releasing his tail is one of the signs of the beginning of Ragnarök.
