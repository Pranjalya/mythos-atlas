# The Mahabharata & Kurukshetra War

**Culture**: Vedic & Hindu | **Epoch**: -800 to 400
**Archetype**: Dynastic Apocalypse / The Cosmic Wheel (Dharma)

## Scholarly Synopsis

The Mahābhārata is one of the two major Sanskrit epics of ancient India revered in Hinduism, the other being the Ramayana. Both are considered smṛti and itihasa texts. The Mahābhārata narrates the events around the Kurukshetra War, while also discussing philosophical concepts, such as the four "goals of life" or puruṣārtha. A principal element of the Mahābhārata is the Bhagavad Gita, which is a prominent sacred text in Hindu traditions.
