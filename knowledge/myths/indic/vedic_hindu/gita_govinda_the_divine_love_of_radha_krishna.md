# Gita Govinda (The Divine Love of Radha & Krishna)

**Culture**: Vedic & Hindu | **Epoch**: 1100 to 1500
**Archetype**: Mystic Devotional Love / Soul Longing for the Supreme Divinity

## Scholarly Synopsis

The Gita Govinda is a work composed by the 12th-century Hindu poet, Jayadeva. It describes the relationship between Krishna, Radha and gopis of Vrindavan.
