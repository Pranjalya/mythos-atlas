# Dasharatha's Ashvamedha and the Divine Incarnation at Ayodhya

**Culture**: Vedic & Hindu | **Epoch**: -800 to 200
**Archetype**: Royal Sacrificial Yajna Summoning the Golden Vessel of Divine Avatars

## Scholarly Synopsis

Dasharatha was the king of Kosala, with its capital at Ayodhya, in the Hindu epic Ramayana. Dasharatha married Kaushalya, Sumitra and Kaikeyi. He was the father of Rama, the protagonist of the epic Ramayana, Bharata, Lakshmana, and Shatrughna. Dasharatha also finds mention in the Vishnu Purana.
