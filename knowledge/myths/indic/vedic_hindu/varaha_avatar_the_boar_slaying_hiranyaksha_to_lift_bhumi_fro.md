# Varaha Avatar (The Boar Slaying Hiranyaksha to Lift Bhumi from the Abyss)

**Culture**: Vedic & Hindu | **Epoch**: -1200 to -400
**Archetype**: Primal Earth-Diver Diving into Cosmic Mud to Elevate the Continent

## Scholarly Synopsis

Varaha is navatar of the Hindu god Vishnu, in the form of a boar. Varaha is generally listed as third in the Dashavatara, the ten principal avatars of Vishnu.
