# The Benben Stone (The Primordial Island Rising from Nun)

**Culture**: Egyptian | **Epoch**: -2500 to -1000
**Archetype**: First Pyramidal Mound of Earth Piercing Primeval Waters upon which Sunlight Broke

## Scholarly Synopsis

In the creation myth of the Heliopolitan form of ancient Egyptian religion,
Benben was the mound that arose from the primordial waters (Nu), and upon which the creator deity Atum settled. The Benben stone is associated with the top stone of a pyramid, which is called a pyramid's pyramidion. It is also related to the obelisk.
