# The Amduat (Ra's Night Journey through the Twelve Hours of the Netherworld)

**Culture**: Egyptian | **Epoch**: -1500 to -1000
**Archetype**: Solar Bark Sailing the Subterranean River Battling Apep

## Scholarly Synopsis

The Amduat is an important ancient Egyptian funerary text of the New Kingdom of Egypt. Similar to previous funerary texts, such as the Old Kingdom's Pyramid Texts, or the First Intermediate Period's Coffin Texts, the Amduat was found carved on the internal walls of a pharaoh's tomb. Unlike other funerary texts, however, it was reserved almost exclusively for pharaohs until the Twenty-first Dynasty, or very select nobility.
