# The Contendings of Horus and Seth (The Trial for the Throne of Egypt)

**Culture**: Egyptian | **Epoch**: -1180 to -1070
**Archetype**: Divine Judicial Combat between Order (Ma'at) and Chaos (Isfet)

## Scholarly Synopsis

"The Contendings of Horus and Seth" is a mythological story from the Twentieth Dynasty of Egypt found in the first sixteen pages of the Chester Beatty Papyri and deals with the battles between Horus and Seth to determine who will succeed Osiris as king.

## Comparative Folkloric Analysis

This narrative crystallizes the **Divine Judicial Combat between Order (Ma'at) and Chaos (Isfet)** archetype within the Egyptian horizon.
