# Ra's Solar Bark through the Duat

**Culture**: Egyptian | **Epoch**: -2600 to -300
**Archetype**: Nocturnal Solar Journey vs Apep Serpent

## Scholarly Synopsis

Ra or Re was the ancient Egyptian deity of the Sun. By the Fifth Dynasty, in the 25th and 24th centuries BC, Ra had become one of the most important gods in ancient Egyptian religion, identified primarily with the noon-day Sun. Ra ruled in all parts of the created world: the sky, the Earth, and the Duat (underworld). He was believed to have ruled as the first pharaoh of Ancient Egypt. He was the god of the Sun, order, kings and the sky.
