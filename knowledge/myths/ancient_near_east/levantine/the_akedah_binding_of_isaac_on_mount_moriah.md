# The Akedah (Binding of Isaac on Mount Moriah)

**Culture**: Levantine | **Epoch**: -1000 to 300
**Archetype**: Supreme Test of Patriarchal Faith and Angelic Intervention at the Altar

## Scholarly Synopsis

The Binding of Isaac, or simply "The Binding", is a story from chapter 22 of the Book of Genesis in the Old Testament. In the Biblical narrative, God orders Abraham to sacrifice his son Isaac on the mountain called Jehovah-jireh in the region of Moriah. As Abraham begins to comply, having bound Isaac to an altar, he is stopped by the Angel of the Lord; a ram appears and is slaughtered in Isaac's stead, as God commends Abraham's pious obedience to offer his son as a human sacrifice.
