# Genesis Deluge (Noah's Ark)

**Culture**: Levantine | **Epoch**: -1000 to -400
**Archetype**: Deluge / Moral Renewal

## Scholarly Synopsis

The Genesis flood narrative is a Hebrew flood myth. It tells of God's decision to destroy creation, saving only Noah and the people and animals who went with him into an ark built on God's instructions.

## Comparative Folkloric Analysis

This narrative crystallizes the **Deluge / Moral Renewal** archetype within the Levantine horizon.
Key structuralist dialectics include:
- **Duality**: Cosmic Chaos (Waters) vs. Sacred Order (Covenant)
