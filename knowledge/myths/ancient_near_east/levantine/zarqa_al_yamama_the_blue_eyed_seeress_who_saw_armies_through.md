# Zarqa al-Yamama (The Blue-Eyed Seeress Who Saw Armies Through Forests)

**Culture**: Levantine | **Epoch**: 300 to 600
**Archetype**: Tragic Prophetess of Infallible Sight Unheeded by Her Doomed People

## Scholarly Synopsis

Zarqa al-Yamama was a legendary blue-eyed woman from the al-Yamama region who lived in pre-Islamic Arabia. She belonged to the Jadis tribe and was said to have had exceptional intuition, sharp eyesight, and ability to predict events before they occurred.
