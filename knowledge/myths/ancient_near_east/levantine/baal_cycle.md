# Baal Cycle

**Culture**: Levantine | **Epoch**: -1400 to -1200
**Archetype**: Storm God vs Mot (Death) & Yam (Sea)

## Scholarly Synopsis

The Baal Cycle is an Ugaritic text about the Canaanite god Baʿal, a storm god associated with fertility. It consists of six tablets, itemized as KTU 1.1–1.6. Tablets one and two are about the cosmic battle between the storm-god Baal and the sea god Yam, where the former attains victory. The next two tablets describe the construction of Baal's palace that marks his cosmic kingship. The last two tablets describe Baal's struggles against Mot, the god of the underworld.
