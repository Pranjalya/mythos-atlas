# Etana's Ascent to Heaven on the Eagle

**Culture**: Mesopotamian | **Epoch**: -2800 to -1600
**Archetype**: Heavenly Ascent / Quest for the Plant of Birth

## Scholarly Synopsis

Etana was the thirteenth king of the first dynasty of Kish, according to the Sumerian King List. He is listed as the successor of Arwium, the son of Mashda, as king of Kish. The list also calls Etana "the shepherd, who ascended to heaven and consolidated all the foreign countries", and states that he ruled 1,500 years before being succeeded by his son Balih, said to have ruled 400 years. The kings on the early part of the SKL are usually not considered historical, except when they are mentioned in contemporary Early Dynastic documents. Etana is one of them.
