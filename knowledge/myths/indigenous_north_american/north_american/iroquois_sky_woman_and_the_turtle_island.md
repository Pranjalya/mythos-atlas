# Iroquois Sky Woman and the Turtle Island

**Culture**: North American Indigenous | **Epoch**: 1000 to 1500
**Archetype**: Pregnant Sky Woman Falling to Earth / World Built on Turtle's Back

## Scholarly Synopsis

Haudenosaunee mythology, or Iroquois mythology, includes the creation stories and other religious oral literature of the Haudenosaunee Confederacy composed of six nations of Indigenous peoples of the Northeastern Woodlands in Canada and the United States. Five nations, the Cayuga, Onondaga, Oneida, Mohawk, and Seneca, founded the confederacy. They were joined by the sixth nation, the Tuscarora, in the 18th century.
