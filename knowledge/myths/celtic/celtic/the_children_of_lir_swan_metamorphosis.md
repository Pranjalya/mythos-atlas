**Culture**: Celtic | **Epoch**: 500 to 1200
**Archetype**: Jealous Stepmother Curse / 900 Years of Metamorphosis

## Scholarly Synopsis

The Children of Lir is a legend from Irish mythology. It is a tale from the post-Christianisation period that mixes magical elements such as wands and spells with a Christian message of faith bringing freedom from suffering.
