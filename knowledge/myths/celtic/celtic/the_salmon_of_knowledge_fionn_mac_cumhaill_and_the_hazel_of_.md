# The Salmon of Knowledge (Fionn mac Cumhaill and the Hazel of Wisdom)

**Culture**: Celtic | **Epoch**: 700 to 1200
**Archetype**: Tasting the Sacred Fish to Awaken Omniscience through Thumb-Chewing

## Scholarly Synopsis

The Salmon of Knowledge is a creature in the Fenian Cycle of Irish mythology, sometimes identified with the mythical seer Fintan mac Bóchra who was known as "The Wise" and was once transformed into a salmon.

## Comparative Folkloric Analysis

This narrative crystallizes the **Tasting the Sacred Fish to Awaken Omniscience through Thumb-Chewing** archetype within the Celtic horizon.
