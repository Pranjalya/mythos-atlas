# Oisín in Tír na nÓg (Land of Eternal Youth)

**Culture**: Celtic | **Epoch**: 300 to 1400
**Archetype**: Otherworldly Paradise / Fatal Time Dilation

## Scholarly Synopsis

In Irish mythology, Tír na nÓg or Tír na hÓige is one of the names for the Celtic Otherworld. Tír na nÓg is best known from the tale of Oisín and Niamh.
