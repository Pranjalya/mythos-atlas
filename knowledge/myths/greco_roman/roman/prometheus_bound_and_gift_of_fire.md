# Prometheus Bound and Gift of Fire

**Culture**: Greco-Roman | **Epoch**: -750 to -400
**Archetype**: Trickster Titan / Theft of Divine Light

## Scholarly Synopsis

In Greek mythology, Prometheus is a Titan who is responsible for creating and aiding humanity in its earliest days. He defied the Olympian gods by stealing fire from them and giving it to humanity.
