# Deucalion and Pyrrha (The Deluge on Mount Parnassus)

**Culture**: Greco-Roman | **Epoch**: -800 to -300
**Archetype**: Cosmic Flood Survivors Re-creating Humanity by Casting the Bones of Mother Earth

## Scholarly Synopsis

In Greek mythology, Deucalion was the son of Prometheus; ancient sources name his mother as Clymene, Hesione, or Pronoia. He is closely connected with a flood myth in Greek mythology.

## Comparative Folkloric Analysis

This narrative crystallizes the **Cosmic Flood Survivors Re-creating Humanity by Casting the Bones of Mother Earth** archetype within the Greco-Roman horizon.
