# Pygmalion and Galatea (The Ivory Maiden Brought to Breath)

**Culture**: Greco-Roman | **Epoch**: -500 to 100
**Archetype**: Artist Breathing Animated Life into the Perfect Sculpted Form

## Scholarly Synopsis

In classical mythology, Pygmalion was a legendary Phoenician figure of Cyprus. He is most familiar from Ovid's narrative poem Metamorphoses, in which Pygmalion was a sculptor who fell in love with a statue he had carved.
