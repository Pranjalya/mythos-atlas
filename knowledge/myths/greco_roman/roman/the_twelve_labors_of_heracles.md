# The Twelve Labors of Heracles

**Culture**: Greco-Roman | **Epoch**: -900 to -200
**Archetype**: Archetypal Demigod / Twelve Cosmic Labors and Apotheosis

## Scholarly Synopsis

Heracles, born Alcaeus or Alcides, was a divine hero in Greek mythology, the son of Zeus and Alcmene, and the foster son of Amphitryon. He was a descendant of Perseus, another son of Zeus.
