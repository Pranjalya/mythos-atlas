# Medea and the Golden Fleece (The Sorceress of Colchis and the Dragon Chariot)

**Culture**: Greco-Roman | **Epoch**: -700 to -400
**Archetype**: Wild Foreign Sorceress Aiding the Quest Only to Wreak Ferocious Revenge

## Scholarly Synopsis

In ancient Greek mythology, Medea is the daughter of King Aeëtes of Colchis. Medea is known in most stories as a sorceress, an accomplished pharmakís, a worker in pharmakeía, and is often depicted as a high priestess of the goddess Hecate. She is a mythical granddaughter of the sun god Helios and a niece of Circe, an enchantress goddess. Her mother may have been Idyia.
