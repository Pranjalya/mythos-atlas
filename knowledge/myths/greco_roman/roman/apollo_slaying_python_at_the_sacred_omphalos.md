# Apollo Slaying Python at the Sacred Omphalos

**Culture**: Greco-Roman | **Epoch**: -800 to -300
**Archetype**: Solar God Slaying Chthonic Earth Serpent to Establish the Oracle

## Scholarly Synopsis

In Greek mythology, Python was the serpent, sometimes represented as a medieval-style dragon, living at the center of the Earth, believed by the ancient Greeks to be at Delphi. He was killed by the god Apollo.
