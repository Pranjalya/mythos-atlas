# The Fall of Icarus and the Labyrinth of Minos (Wings of Wax)

**Culture**: Greco-Roman | **Epoch**: -700 to -300
**Archetype**: Hubris of Human Flight Soaring Too Near the Blazing Sun

## Scholarly Synopsis

In Greek mythology, Icarus was the son of the master craftsman Daedalus, the architect of the labyrinth of Crete, and his wife Naucrate. After Theseus, king of Athens and enemy of King Minos, escaped from the labyrinth, Minos suspected that Icarus and Daedalus had revealed the labyrinth's secrets and thus imprisoned them—either in a large tower overlooking the ocean or in the labyrinth itself, depending upon the account.
