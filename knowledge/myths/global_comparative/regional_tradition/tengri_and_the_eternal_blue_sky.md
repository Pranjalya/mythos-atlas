# Tengri and the Eternal Blue Sky

**Culture**: Central Asian & Steppe | **Epoch**: -1000 to 1300
**Archetype**: Supreme Celestial Sky Father & Shamanic Tree

## Scholarly Synopsis

Tengrism is a belief system originating in the Eurasian steppes, based on shamanism and animism, and commonly centered on the titular sky god Tengri. According to some scholars, adherents of Tengrism view the purpose of life as living in harmony with the universe.
