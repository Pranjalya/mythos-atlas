# Louhi the Mistress of Pohjola (The Shapeshifting Eagle-Hag of the Frozen North)

**Culture**: Finno-Ugric | **Epoch**: 800 to 1849
**Archetype**: Fierce Sorceress Matriarch Defending the Mill of Abundance

## Scholarly Synopsis

Louhi is the ruler of Pohjola in Finnish mythology. She is regarded as a goddess of death and disease. She is also the mother of wolves. Her original role was likely as the ruler of the underworld.

## Comparative Folkloric Analysis
