# Ural-batyr (The Bashkir Epic of the Death-Defying Winged Steed Akbuzat)

**Culture**: Central Asian & Steppe | **Epoch**: 800 to 1800
**Archetype**: Hero Slaying Death and Evil Kings to Pour the Spring of Living Water on Earth

## Scholarly Synopsis

Ural-batyr or Oral-batır is the most famous kubair of the Bashkirs. It is a telling of heroic deeds and legendary creatures, the formation of natural phenomena, and so on.
