# Kintu and Nambi (Buganda Genesis and the Origin of Death)

**Culture**: Central & Southern African | **Epoch**: 1000 to 1500
**Archetype**: First Man Gaining the Earth and the Origin of Death (Walumbe)

## Scholarly Synopsis

Kintu is a mythological figure who appears in a creation myth of the people of Buganda, Uganda. According to this legend, Kintu was the first person on earth, and the first Muganda.

## Comparative Folkloric Analysis

This narrative crystallizes the **First Man Gaining the Earth and the Origin of Death (Walumbe)** archetype within the Central & Southern African horizon.
