# Kanaloa (The Sovereign of the Deepest Ocean Trench and the Giant Squid)

**Culture**: Oceanic & Australasian | **Epoch**: 1000 to 1850
**Archetype**: Cephalopod Sovereign Guiding Navigators Across Boundless Trans-Pacific Voyages

## Scholarly Synopsis

In the traditions of ancient Hawaiʻi, Kanaloa is a god symbolized by the squid or by the octopus, and is typically associated with Kāne. It is also an alternative name for the island of Kahoʻolawe.
