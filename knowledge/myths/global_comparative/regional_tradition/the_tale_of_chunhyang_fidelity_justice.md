# The Tale of Chunhyang (Fidelity & Justice)

**Culture**: East Asian | **Epoch**: 1300 to 1550
**Archetype**: Secret Royal Inspector / Undying Love and Justice

## Scholarly Synopsis

Chunhyangjeon is one of the best known love stories and folk tales of Korea. It is based on the pansori Chunhyangga, the most famous of the five surviving pansori tales.

## Comparative Folkloric Analysis

This narrative crystallizes the **Secret Royal Inspector / Undying Love and Justice** archetype within the East Asian horizon.
