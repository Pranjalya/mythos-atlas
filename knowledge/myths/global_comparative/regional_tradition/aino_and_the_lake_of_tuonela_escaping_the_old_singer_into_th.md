# Aino and the Lake of Tuonela (Escaping the Old Singer into the Waters)

**Culture**: Finno-Ugric | **Epoch**: 800 to 1849
**Archetype**: Maiden Escaping Forced Marriage Becoming the Sacred Salmon of the Sea

## Scholarly Synopsis

Aino is a figure in the Kalevala, a Finnish national epic by Elias Lönnrot. Lönnrot invented the name Aino, meaning "the only one"; the original poems only call her "the only daughter" or "the only sister".
