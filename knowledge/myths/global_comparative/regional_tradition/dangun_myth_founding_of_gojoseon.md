# Dangun Myth (Founding of Gojoseon)

**Culture**: East Asian | **Epoch**: -1000 to 1281
**Archetype**: Bear-Woman (Ungnyeo) and Heavenly Son King

## Scholarly Synopsis

Tan'gun, also known as Tan'gun Wanggŏm, was the legendary founder and first king of Gojoseon, the first Korean kingdom. He founded the first kingdom around the northern part of the Korean Peninsula. He is said to be the "grandson of heaven", "son of a bear", and to have founded the first kingdom in 2333 BC, whose descendants was surname Park or Pai.
