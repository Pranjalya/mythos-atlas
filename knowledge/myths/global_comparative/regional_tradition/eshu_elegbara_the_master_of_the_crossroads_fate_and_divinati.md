# Eshu Elegbara (The Master of the Crossroads, Fate, and Divination)

**Culture**: West African | **Epoch**: 800 to 1900
**Archetype**: Divine Trickster Herald Standing at the Threshold between Mortals and Orishas

## Scholarly Synopsis

Èṣù is the oriṣa of trickery, chance, crossroads, judgement, duality and languages in the Yoruba religion. Eṣu is a prominent primordial divinity who descended from Ìkọ̀lé Ọ̀run, and the chief enforcer of natural and divine laws. He is in charge of law enforcement and orderliness. As the Yoruba religion spread around the world, the name of this Orisha has varied in different locations, but the beliefs remain similar.
