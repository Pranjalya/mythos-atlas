# Alpamysh (The Steppe Hero's Exile, Prison, and Triumphant Return)

**Culture**: Central Asian & Steppe | **Epoch**: 800 to 1500
**Archetype**: Exiled Hero Escaping Dungeon to Reclaim His Faithful Bride at Archery Contest

## Scholarly Synopsis

Alpamysh, also spelled as Alpamish or Alpamis, is an ancient Turkic epic or dastan, an ornate oral history, generally set in verse, and one of the most important examples of the Turkic oral literature of Central Asia, mainly the Kipchak Turks.
