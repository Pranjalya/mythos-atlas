# The Princess of Mount Ledang (Puteri Gunung Ledang)

**Culture**: Southeast Asian | **Epoch**: 1300 to 1500
**Archetype**: Mountain Fairy Demanding Bridge of Gold and Tears

## Scholarly Synopsis

The legend revolves around a celestial princess who lived on Mount Ledang, located in present-day Tangkak District, Johor, Malaysia.
