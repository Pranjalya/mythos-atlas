# Chaac the Rain God of Sacred Cenotes

**Culture**: Mesoamerican | **Epoch**: 300 to 1200
**Archetype**: Lightning Axe Strike Shattering Clouds over the Cenote

## Scholarly Synopsis

Chaac is the name of the Maya god of rain, thunder, and lightning. With his lightning axe, Chaac strikes the clouds, causing them to produce thunder and rain. Chaac corresponds to Tlaloc among the Aztecs and Cocijo among the Zapotecs.
