# Daramulum (The Emu-Footed Son of Baiame and the Sound of the Bullroarer)

**Culture**: Oceanic & Australasian | **Epoch**: -4000 to 1850
**Archetype**: Initiation Deity Whose Roaring Voice Echoes in the Sacred Whirling Bullroarer

## Scholarly Synopsis

In Aboriginal cultures of south-east Australia, Daramulum is a sky hero associated with Baiame, and an emu-wife. He is a shapeshifter.
