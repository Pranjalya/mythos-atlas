# Hikayat Hang Tuah (The Indomitable Malay Champion of the Keris Taming Sari)

**Culture**: Southeast Asian | **Epoch**: 1450 to 1700
**Archetype**: Tragic Conflict between Unconditional Fealty to the Throne and Fraternal Bond

## Scholarly Synopsis

Hang Tuah was a Malay warrior and admiral who purportedly lived in Malacca during the reign of Sultan Mansur Shah in the 15th century.
