# Mami Wata (The Serpentine Queen of the Coastal Atlantic and Inland Swells)

**Culture**: West African | **Epoch**: 1400 to 1950
**Archetype**: Water Goddess Entwined with Serpent Holding the Balance of Wealth and Peril

## Scholarly Synopsis

Mami Wata or similar is a mermaid, water spirit, and/or goddess in the folklore of parts of Western Africa, Central Africa, Eastern Africa, and Southern Africa. Historically, scholars trace her origins to early encounters between Europeans and West Africans in the 15th century, where Mami Wata developed from depictions of European mermaids. Mami Wata subsequently joined native pantheons of deities and spirits in parts of Africa.
