# Quetzalcoatl's Descent into Mictlan for the Bones of Humanity

**Culture**: Mesoamerican | **Epoch**: 200 to 1500
**Archetype**: Katabasis for Ancestral Bones / Resurrected by Blood of Quetzalcoatl

## Scholarly Synopsis

Mictlan is the underworld of Aztec mythology. Most people who die would travel to Mictlan, although other possibilities exist. Mictlan consists of nine distinct levels.

## Comparative Folkloric Analysis

This narrative crystallizes the **Katabasis for Ancestral Bones / Resurrected by Blood of Quetzalcoatl** archetype within the Mesoamerican horizon.
