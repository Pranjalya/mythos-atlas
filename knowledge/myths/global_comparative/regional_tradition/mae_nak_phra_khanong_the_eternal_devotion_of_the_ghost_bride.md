# Mae Nak Phra Khanong (The Eternal Devotion of the Ghost Bride of Bangkok)

**Culture**: Southeast Asian | **Epoch**: 1650 to 1850
**Archetype**: Loving Ghost Mother Caring for Unsuspecting Husband from Beyond the Grave

## Scholarly Synopsis

Mae Nak Phra Khanong, or simply Mae Nak or Nang Nak, is a well-known Thai ghost. According to local folklore the story is based on events that took place during the reign of King Rama IV.
