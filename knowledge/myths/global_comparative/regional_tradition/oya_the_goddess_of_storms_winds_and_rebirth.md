# Oya the Goddess of Storms, Winds, and Rebirth

**Culture**: West African | **Epoch**: 800 to 1500
**Archetype**: Fierce Wind Warrior Guiding the Souls across Rebirth

## Scholarly Synopsis

Ọya is one of the principal female deities of the Yoruba pantheon. She is the oriṣa of winds, lightning, and storms and is the only oriṣa capable of controlling the Eégún, a power given to her by Babalú Ayé.
