# Nezha Conquering the Dragon Kings of the Four Seas

**Culture**: East Asian | **Epoch**: 600 to 1550
**Archetype**: Lotus Reborn Warrior Hero / Wind Fire Wheels & Universe Ring

## Scholarly Synopsis

Nezha or sometimes Nezha the Crown Prince, or Natha, is a protection deity in Taoism, Buddhism, Confucianism, and Chinese folk religion. His official Taoist name is "Marshal of the Central Altar" (中壇元帥). He was then given the title "Third Lotus Prince" (蓮花三太子) after he became a deity.
