# Pangu and the Primordial Cosmic Egg

**Culture**: East Asian | **Epoch**: -200 to 500
**Archetype**: Cosmic Giant Self-Sacrifice to form Earth

## Scholarly Synopsis

Pangu or Pan Gu 
(Chinese: 盤古; pinyin: Pángǔ ; PAN-koo) is a primordial being and creation figure in Chinese mythology and in Taoism. According to legend, Pangu separated heaven and earth, and his body later became geographic features such as mountains and flowing water.
