# Kalunga (The Great Ocean Horizon Boundary between the Living and Ancestors)

**Culture**: Central & Southern African | **Epoch**: 1200 to 1900
**Archetype**: Vast Watery Mirror Separating the White Ancestral Realm from the Physical World

## Scholarly Synopsis

The Kalûnga Line is a foundational concept in West-Central African Bantu cosmologies—most prominently within Ambundu, Kongo, and Chokwe traditions—referring both to Kalûnga as the Supreme God and to the watery, energetic boundary separating the land of the living from the spiritual realm of deceased ancestors.
