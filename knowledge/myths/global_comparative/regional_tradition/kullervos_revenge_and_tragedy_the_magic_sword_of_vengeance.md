# Kullervo's Revenge and Tragedy (The Magic Sword of Vengeance)

**Culture**: Finno-Ugric | **Epoch**: 800 to 1849
**Archetype**: Cursed Orphan of Unbreakable Will Driven into Inevitable Doom

## Scholarly Synopsis

Kullervo is a hero in Finnish and Estonian mythology. He is often called a son of Kaleva. He also appears as an ill-fated character in the epic Kalevala by Elias Lönnrot.
