# Laima and the Weaving of Human Destiny

**Culture**: Slavic & Baltic | **Epoch**: 500 to 1400
**Archetype**: Baltic Fate Goddess Weaving the Sacred Life Thread

## Scholarly Synopsis

Laima is a Baltic goddess of fate. She was associated with childbirth, marriage, and death; she was also the patron of pregnant women.
