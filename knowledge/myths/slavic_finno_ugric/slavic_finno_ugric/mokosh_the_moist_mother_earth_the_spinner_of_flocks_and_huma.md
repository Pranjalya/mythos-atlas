# Mokosh the Moist Mother Earth (The Spinner of Flocks and Human Destiny)

**Culture**: Slavic & Baltic | **Epoch**: 600 to 1200
**Archetype**: Moist Earth Matron Spinning the Thread of Mortal Fate

## Scholarly Synopsis

Mokosh is a Slavic goddess. No narratives about this deity have survived: Mokosh was mentioned in various sermons against Paganism along with the vilas, but is not described by them, and all modern descriptions are reconstructions.
