# Baba Yaga in the Hut on Fowl's Legs

**Culture**: Slavic & Baltic | **Epoch**: 600 to 1500
**Archetype**: Threshold Guardian Witch / Forest Initiator

## Scholarly Synopsis

Baba Yaga is a female character from Slavic folklore who has two contrasting roles. In some narratives, she is described as a repulsive or ferocious-looking old woman who fries and eats children, while in others she is depicted as a nice old woman who helps the hero. Both versions often depict her as a witch. She is often associated with forest wildlife. Her distinctive traits are flying around in a wooden mortar, wielding a pestle, and dwelling deep in the forest in a hut with chicken legs.
