# Sadko the Merchant-Guslar of Novgorod (The Underwater Feast of the Sea Tsar)

**Culture**: Slavic & Baltic | **Epoch**: 1000 to 1400
**Archetype**: Minstrel Whose Music Charms the Sovereign of the Waves

## Scholarly Synopsis

Sadko is a principal character in Russian byliny. He is an adventurer, merchant, and gusli musician from Novgorod. The story of Sadko is best known outside Russia in the opera Sadko by Nikolai Rimsky-Korsakov.
