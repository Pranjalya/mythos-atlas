# The Firebird and Tsarevich Ivan

**Culture**: Slavic & Baltic | **Epoch**: 900 to 1400
**Archetype**: Luminous Solar Avian Quest / Allied Grey Wolf

## Scholarly Synopsis

In Slavic mythology and folklore, the
Firebird is a magical and prophetic glowing or burning bird from a faraway land which is both a blessing and a harbinger of doom to its captor.
