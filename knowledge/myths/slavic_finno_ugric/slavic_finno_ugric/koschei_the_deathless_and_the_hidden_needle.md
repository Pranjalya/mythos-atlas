# Koschei the Deathless and the Hidden Needle

**Culture**: Slavic & Baltic | **Epoch**: 900 to 1400
**Archetype**: External Soul (Egg in Duck in Hare in Oak Chest)

## Scholarly Synopsis

Koshchei, also Kashchei, often given the epithet "the Immortal", or "the Deathless", is an archetypal male antagonist in Russian folklore and mythology.

## Comparative Folkloric Analysis

This narrative crystallizes the **External Soul (Egg in Duck in Hare in Oak Chest)** archetype within the Slavic & Baltic horizon.
