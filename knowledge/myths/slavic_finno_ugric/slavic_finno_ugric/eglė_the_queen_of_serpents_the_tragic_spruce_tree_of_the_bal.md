# Eglė the Queen of Serpents (The Tragic Spruce Tree of the Baltic Shore)

**Culture**: Slavic & Baltic | **Epoch**: 800 to 1850

## Scholarly Synopsis

Eglė the Queen of Serpents, alternatively Eglė the Queen of Grass Snakes, is a Lithuanian folk tale, first published was a translation by M. Jasevičius in 1837.
